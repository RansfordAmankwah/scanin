Questions, answered.

Can a student check a friend in?

It's hard. Codes expire every 30 seconds, each check-in is tied to the student's device, and one phone can't check two people into the same class. Anything unusual is flagged for the lecturer, not punished automatically.

Do students need to install an app?

No. ScanIn runs in the browser. Students sign in and scan. Nothing to download.

Is face verification required?

No. It's off by default and enabled per course, only with the student's consent. It's a review signal and never stores a raw image.

Who can use it?

Lecturers create their own courses and sessions; students join by signing in and checking in.
