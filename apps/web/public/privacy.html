Privacy

A short, plain-language summary of how ScanIn handles data. This is the general site notice; signed-in users see the detailed, in-app notices that apply to their account.

What ScanIn is for

ScanIn records class attendance. Lecturers create courses and sessions; students check in by scanning a QR code. Accounts are limited to two roles — lecturer and student.

What we collect

  • Account details you provide: name, e-mail, and, for students, a student ID. Passwords are stored only as salted hashes.
  • Attendance data: which sessions you checked in to, when, and the review trail a lecturer creates for a course.
  • Limited technical data needed to run the service securely, such as a hashed form of IP address for rate limiting and a device reference tied to a check-in.

What we deliberately do not do

  • We do not collect your precise location.
  • We do not write anything to official university or examination systems.
  • We do not use face verification unless a course explicitly turns it on and you consent; even then, no raw image is stored. A separate notice covers that pilot in detail.

How long we keep it

Attendance and audit data are kept for a defined retention window and then purged. Security logs are minimised and kept only as long as needed.

Your choices

You can sign in to view and export your own attendance. For access, correction or deletion requests, or any privacy question, contact us at privacy@scanin.dev.

This notice may be updated as ScanIn develops; material changes will be reflected here.
